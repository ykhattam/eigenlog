# Eigenlog

A polyglot epistemic engine (Python, SQL, Bash) for mapping concepts, logging reflections, and tracking unknown unknowns.

It keeps a map of categories, a log of reflections, a register of claims with honest statuses, and a record of what did not fit the map. It also asks the same questions in SQL and the shell.

It cannot detect unknown unknowns, because a lookup cannot find what is not yet named. It records the symptoms: concepts with no category, surprises, and a residual list that keeps growing.

## The Post
I practised Python and pandas with Claude. It turned out to be a good tool for reflection too.

I practise my languages so I do not lose the threads between meanings. I reflect regularly because it helps me make sense of an uncertain world.

Writing, painting, sport, conversation and singing are also ways of expressing. They carry weight that logic does not.

Logic clarifies the frameworks we think in. It cannot supply values. My working formula: a definition plus its applicability is wisdom, and applicability is where values come in.

Careful analysis cuts some errors, such as unclear words and inconsistent claims. It does not cut wrong premises or missing data.

So I built a small map of my own categories. It will not tell me why we exist. It is meant to help me navigate. The log is empty until I add real days, so I have no conclusions yet. Once I do, they will be inductive: probable, not proven. Deduction only tests them.

That leaves a question I cannot settle with the same tools: what shapes the lenses I use?

I also post because some of my writing drew interest. I appreciate that, and I value clarity.
